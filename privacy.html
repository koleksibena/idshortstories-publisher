Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Operated by Bena Content

This Privacy Policy explains how IDShortstories Publisher ("the Application"), operated by Bena Content, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when an authorized creator connects a TikTok account and uploads videos through TikTok's official APIs.

1. Information We Access via TikTok APIs

When you authorize your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit for Desktop (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE), the Application requests only the following scopes and data:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Your TikTok open_id, union_id, display name, and avatar URL, used solely to confirm which TikTok account is connected in the local publishing interface.
  • Creator Publishing Capabilities (video.publish): Creator nickname, username, allowed privacy level options, interaction availability (Comment, Duet, Stitch), and maximum video duration returned by TikTok's creator_info/query endpoint prior to posting.
  • Video Upload & Publishing (video.upload, video.publish): Local video files, captions, and explicit publishing settings chosen by the creator, transmitted directly from the creator's workstation to TikTok's upload servers, along with resulting publish status IDs.
  • OAuth Tokens: Access tokens, refresh tokens, and token expiration timestamps returned by TikTok's token endpoint.

2. How We Use Information

  • Display the connected TikTok account name and avatar so the creator verifies the destination profile before uploading.
  • Validate video duration and enforce account-specific privacy and interaction options before initializing a post.
  • Upload creator-approved MP4 files directly to TikTok (either as Direct Posts or Inbox Drafts) and check upload processing status.
  • Maintain a local upload log (campaign, part, timestamp, and publish ID) on the creator's workstation to avoid duplicate uploads.

3. Storage & Security

IDShortstories Publisher operates locally on the authorized creator's desktop workstation. OAuth credentials, tokens (.tiktok_tokens.json), and upload logs (.tiktok_history.json) are stored strictly locally on the user's device and are never uploaded to external databases, analytics services, or third-party servers. All communication with TikTok APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS (TLS).

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your TikTok account information, tokens, or media with any third party. Data is transmitted exclusively between your local workstation and official TikTok API endpoints (www.tiktok.com, open.tiktokapis.com, and TikTok's signed media upload endpoints) to perform actions you explicitly initiate.

5. Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Access

Tokens and local upload logs remain on your workstation only for as long as you use the Application. You can delete all stored data at any time by removing .tiktok_tokens.json and .tiktok_history.json from your local workspace, and you can immediately revoke API access from your TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

6. Children's Privacy

IDShortstories Publisher is an internal creator publishing tool intended solely for authorized adult content creators and team members of Bena Content, and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction).

7. Updates to This Policy & Contact

We may revise this Privacy Policy as needed to stay aligned with TikTok developer requirements and applicable privacy laws. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact Bena Content through our official channel at https://www.tiktok.com/@idshortstories.
