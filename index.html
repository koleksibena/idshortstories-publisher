What IDShortstories Publisher Does

Local Campaign Sync

Reads rendered vertical 9:16 MP4 episodes, captions, hashtags, and series metadata directly from our local studio campaign folders without third-party cloud intermediaries.

Verified Creator Controls

Queries TikTok creator capabilities before every upload, validating video duration limits and presenting explicit privacy, comment, duet, stitch, and commercial disclosure settings.

Direct Post & Draft Inbox

Supports both TikTok Direct Post (video.publish) and Draft Inbox transfer (video.upload) using secure chunked FILE_UPLOAD delivery and real-time status tracking.

How the Publishing Workflow Works

  1. Secure TikTok OAuth Sign-In (Desktop PKCE): The authorized channel creator connects their TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit for Desktop using OAuth 2.0 with PKCE.
  2. Episode Selection & Preview: The creator selects a finished episode from the local production workspace, previews the video, and reviews or edits the caption and hashtags.
  3. Explicit Publishing Settings: Before publishing, the studio fetches live creator permissions and requires the creator to manually select visibility, interaction permissions (Comment, Duet, Stitch), and commercial content or AI-generated disclosures.
  4. Upload & Status Confirmation: After explicit confirmation of TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation, the MP4 file is uploaded in chunks via TikTok Content Posting API v2 and monitored until completion.

TikTok API Scopes & Data Principles

IDShortstories Publisher requests only the minimum TikTok permissions required to identify the connected creator account and publish creator-approved videos:

user.info.basic video.upload video.publish

OAuth tokens are stored exclusively on the creator's local workstation. We do not sell, rent, or share creator data with third parties, and access can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings. For full details, read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
